# Tradecraft: a strategy guide

None of these are rules or buttons. They all fall out of the few rules there are. The same advice
is in the in-game Field Manual.

- **Canary trap.** Tell two suspect agents two different stories ("Bern on Thursday" / "Lisbon on
  Friday"). Whichever story the opposition acts on names the agent who is talking.
- **Chicken feed.** Once you know an agent is turned, don't burn them. Keep cabling them, with lies.
  Their cables reach Karla word for word, so make every one count.
- **Cross-check.** Have two agents Watch the same city. If the reports disagree, one of them isn't yours.
- **Sequencing.** A city with a Ferret is a trap for Scalphunters. Send a Lamplighter to Watch, then
  Expose the Ferret, then strike. Exposes resolve before strikes, so the last two can happen the same night.
- **Ferrets notice watchers.** Watching a guarded city tells the other side who is looking. Expect a
  Scalphunter to come for your Lamplighter next.
- **Bait.** A city that looks empty invites a blind strike. Make sure your Ferret is there when it comes.
- **Brevity.** "Vienna. Usual arrangement." is safe if it leaks, and easy to misread if it doesn't.
  Name targets only when you must.
- **Failed strikes are information.** If your Scalphunter keeps "finding no target" where a report
  said one would be, ask who wrote that report, or ask whether your Scalphunter is still yours.
- **Feed your sources.** If you hold a source inside their network, cable it to dictate its next
  report home. Say a city is empty when your Ferret is waiting there.
